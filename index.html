Ratio & Vector Principles

To eliminate camera distance errors and size variance, FACET strictly uses normalized distance ratios ($d_1 / d_2$) and angular vectors. No universal facial width in millimeters is assumed.


Comparison Score Scale

  • 0.00: Exactly at the midpoint of the clinical reference interval.
  • 1.00: Positioned at the outer boundary of the reference interval.
  • > 1.00: Outside standard reference boundaries.
